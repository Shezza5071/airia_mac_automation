The Challenge

Getting a new client into AlayaCare was
slow, manual and error prone.

Complex Source Documents

MAC snapshots are dense, multi page PDFs covering client details, care approvals, support networks, assessment history and more, across up to 8 pages per record.

Manual Re-entry

Coordinators had to read each field from the PDF and retype it into AlayaCare. Slow, repetitive work with a high risk of transcription errors and no audit trail.

Data Does Not Map Directly

Many MAC fields need splitting, reformatting or interpretation to match AlayaCare's field types and dropdown constraints. Impossible to automate without AI.

The AI Advantage

Why this could not be done without AI

Every MAC Document Is Different

No two MAC PDFs are formatted identically. Addresses use different notation, fields appear in different orders, and values are expressed differently across documents. AI adapts to each one. A rigid script would break.

Handles all MAC formatting variations

Dropdown Values Require Judgment

AlayaCare's fields often require a specific code from a defined list. The source data rarely matches exactly. AI applies a set of business rules to select the correct value, something that cannot be hardcoded.

25+ mapped dropdown fields

Consistent and Validated Every Time

The AI follows a strict set of extraction rules on every run. Dates are always converted to the correct format, phone numbers are normalised, and required fields are never left blank without a reason.

Strict output schema enforced on every record

Scales Without Adding Headcount

Whether processing one record or fifty, the pipeline takes the same time and effort per record. No training, no fatigue, no variability. The same quality output every single time.

Seconds per record regardless of volume

What's Next

Pipeline is live on UAT. Four enhancements on the roadmap.

Enhancement 1

Accounting Field Completion

Map the remaining two fields, Home Support classification and Funding Approval Date, into AlayaCare's Support At Home accounting section. The AI already extracts both values. One additional step is required in the pipeline.

One additional pipeline step
Enhancement 2

Attach PDF to Client Record

Automatically upload the original MAC PDF into the AlayaCare client documents section, so the source document is always accessible alongside the record for reference and auditing.

Document storage integration
Enhancement 3

Human in the Loop Approval

Before a record is committed, a coordinator reviews the AI extracted data, corrects any fields, and explicitly approves the import. A human remains accountable for every new client created.

Review checkpoint before commit
Enhancement 4

Zendesk Ticket Audit Trail

Link the originating Zendesk ticket to each completed import. A full audit trail shows who requested it, which document was used, when it was approved and which AlayaCare record was created.

Compliance and accountability
Ongoing

Expand Fields as Required

As teams use the pipeline and identify additional fields that would be valuable to populate, the extraction and mapping rules can be updated without rebuilding the pipeline from scratch.

Configurable and extendable
